01 — Why the problem

Highways carry a fraction of the road network and most of its cost.

The constraint isn't enforcement will — it's physical coverage. A patrol vehicle watches one stretch of road at a time; everything else is a blind spot until someone reports it.

03 — Response logic

Same drone, same dock — two different protocols.

Highway and security incidents need different postures once the drone is on scene. The site is configured once; every operator at that site follows the right logic automatically.

Highway corridor

Confirm, then commit.

  • Drone arrives, streams live feed to the operator.
  • Operator confirms the incident is genuine before any ground unit moves.
  • Ground unit dispatched with confirmed location and details.
  • Incident logged as resolved on arrival.
Border / security corridor

Move first, confirm live.

  • Ground unit dispatched immediately, in parallel with the drone.
  • Drone holds a continuous live feed to both operator and ground unit.
  • Feed persists through the entire approach — not just arrival.
  • Marked resolved only once the situation is confirmed secured.
